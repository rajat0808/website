About Us

Founded in 2017, Basanti leads the way with contemporary styles that fuse fast fashion with timeless elegance. The brand is dedicated to refreshing your wardrobe with designs that blend modern simplicity with romantic flair, while keeping every piece affordable.

The flagship store elevates the Basanti experience with a charming coffee bar, inviting you to unwind with a cup of coffee and the soothing rhythms of folk India in a serene shopping environment.

Every visit to Basanti offers more than shopping. From ambient music to a thoughtful product range, each detail is curated to enrich your lifestyle.

Whether you are browsing the latest collections, enjoying a quiet coffee, or exploring accessories, Basanti ensures every piece you take home is a testament to quality and style.
