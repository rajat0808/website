About Us

In 2011, all roads led to KORA, a brand built on the fashion values that Nilesh and Mitesh swore by. KORA offers the highest quality traditional wear, exclusively for men. The designs redefine industry standards and fuse traditional crafts with contemporary haute couture in men's fashion.

The journey did not stop there. The designer duo entered the industry at a time when it was divided between super high-end celebrity designers that the common man could not afford or relate to and the unorganized sector that was inexpensive but lacked flair. With KORA, they removed this lack of choice and delivered luxury traditional fashion that was accessible. KORA became the go-to name for the fashion-conscious as well as the quality-conscious.

The designers envisioned a brand that lives beyond their own names and becomes a fashion institution unto itself. Their dream is to make KORA a leading men's fashion brand in India.

Since the company's inception in 2009, KORA has grown from a small retail store in Mumbai that opened its doors in 2011 to a well-known brand with 20 store locations, serving customers across India and abroad. Renowned for high-quality Sherwanis, Kurtas, Suits, and more, KORA blends traditional crafts with contemporary haute couture while bridging the gap between high-end celebrity designers and the unorganized fashion sector by offering luxurious yet affordable traditional wear for men.
