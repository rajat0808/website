About the Brand

Shop Kora by NM in Lucknow

Explore Kora by NM at Sindh Emporio for refined Indian menswear, including kurtas, Indo-western sets, festive ensembles, and occasion-ready separates designed for the modern man.

Kora by NM is a contemporary Indian menswear brand that redefines how the modern man approaches occasionwear. Rooted in traditional craftsmanship yet driven by evolving design sensibilities, Kora creates pieces that feel relevant, effortless, and occasion-ready without being overwhelming. From kurtas and Indo-western sets to elevated festive wear, the brand focuses on clean silhouettes, thoughtful detailing, and versatile styling.

Since its inception, Kora by NM has expanded its footprint across 27+ stores across India, UAE and UK, building a strong retail presence both in India and internationally. The brand continues to grow with a vision to make modern Indian occasionwear more accessible and relevant to today’s consumer. The Lucknow store, launched in March 2025, marks another step in this journey, bringing Kora’s signature aesthetic closer to the city’s culturally rooted yet style-forward audience.

Over the years, Kora by NM has become synonymous with comfort, individuality, and a new-age approach to Indian dressing—made for moments that matter, big or small.
